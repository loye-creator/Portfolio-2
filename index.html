LincRide

Growth Lead

Ride-hailing startup, Lagos  /  3-month engagement

GA4Google Tag ManagerdataLayer specMeta PixelTikTok AdsCreative testing

Took a ride-hailing app from no measurement to 350 installs in under two days.

LincRide was about to spend on acquisition with no way of knowing what it bought. I came in as Growth Lead and built the measurement layer first: GA4 and Google Tag Manager from scratch, a written dataLayer event specification so every install, signup and ride request fired consistently, then Meta and TikTok pixels wired into it.

Only once that was standing did the spend start. I ran the TikTok side of a multi-platform paid launch with a creative testing structure across headline variants, so budget moved toward what was actually converting rather than what looked best in the deck.

The launch put 200 Android and 150 iOS installs on the board inside 48 hours, with the strongest headline variant holding a 1.09% click-through rate.

Orisha of Lagos

Research, workflow design and working demo

Community events business, Lagos

n8nPaystackHMAC verificationGoogle SheetsProcess design

Killed a manual reconciliation habit by building the replacement before quoting for it.

Registration for their monthly events arrived through three doors at once: Selar, WhatsApp and straight bank transfer. Transfers were the problem. Someone asked the attendee for a receipt, opened the bank account to check it, then typed the name onto a list by hand. At 50 to 100 registrations an event, and well above that on the big ones, that is a person doing data entry instead of running an event.

I built the fix as a working demo rather than a slide, and took it back before pricing came up. It verifies rather than trusts: every incoming payment notification is checked against an HMAC SHA512 signature before anything is written, so a forged webhook cannot add a name to the list.

One detail worth keeping. The first version used Node's crypto module inside a code node and the n8n sandbox refused it, so the chain was rebuilt on the native Crypto node. The notification channel moved too, from a Twilio WhatsApp sandbox that wanted paid template approval, to email. Confirmed end to end against a live Paystack test payment.
