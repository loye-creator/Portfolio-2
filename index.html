LincRide

Growth Lead

Ride-hailing startup, Lagos / 3-month engagement

GA4GTMdataLayerMeta PixelTikTok Ads

Measurement first, then spend.

LincRide was about to spend on acquisition with no way of knowing what it bought. I came in as Growth Lead and built the measurement layer first: GA4 and Google Tag Manager from scratch, a written dataLayer event specification so every install, signup and ride request fired consistently, then Meta and TikTok pixels wired into it.

Only once that was standing did the spend start. I ran the TikTok side of a multi-platform launch with a creative testing structure across headline variants, so budget moved toward what was converting rather than what looked best in the deck.

CASE 04

Alabaster Jar Perfumes

Build and ongoing maintenance

Fragrance e-commerce, United Arab Emirates / live

E-commerceCustom themeZoho Mail

Trade licence to storefront.

The client had a licence, a name and a reference site she admired. Everything else needed building. I scoped the full stack against a fixed tooling budget, weighed a bought premium theme against a custom build and went custom, so the storefront reads as a fragrance house rather than a template with a perfume photo on it.

Business email came with it, on Zoho Mail with role aliases so enquiries do not land in a personal inbox. The engagement continued past launch into a maintenance retainer.

The same pattern is now how I scope Gulf retail work: itemised tooling costs first, build fee separate, maintenance agreed before launch rather than after.

CASE 05

Summerettal

Site architecture and build

Shoot coordination and talent booking, Dubai

Booking flowTalent registrationPositioning

Two audiences, one site, no confusion between them.

Summerettal works in commercial and fashion production out of Dubai, coordinating shoots and booking talent for campaigns including Standard Chartered across the UAE and Thailand, an NYFW editorial and a lifestyle mobility campaign.

The site had to serve two people who want opposite things. Brands need to see the work and book a coordinator. Models need a way in: a registration area where they submit a headshot and a portfolio link without anyone handling attachments by hand. I built the architecture so neither journey interrupts the other, and rewrote the positioning first, because the old copy described a casting agent when the actual offer is shoot coordination and talent booking.

CASE 06

Orisha of Lagos

Research, workflow design, working demo

Community events business, Lagos

n8nPaystackHMACSheets

Built the replacement before quoting for it.

Registration for their monthly events arrived through three doors at once: Selar, WhatsApp and straight bank transfer. Transfers were the problem. Someone asked the attendee for a receipt, opened the bank account to check it, then typed the name onto a list by hand. At 50 to 100 registrations an event, that is a person doing data entry instead of running an event.

I built the fix as a working demo rather than a slide, and took it back before pricing came up. It verifies rather than trusts: every incoming payment notification is checked against an HMAC SHA512 signature before anything is written, so a forged webhook cannot add a name to the list.
