Privacy

Last updated 17 September 2026.

This is a portfolio site. It is deliberately light on data collection, and this page describes all of it in plain terms rather than legal ones.

What the contact form collects

If you send a message, I receive the name, email address, company name and message you typed. I use them for one thing: replying to you and discussing the work. I do not add you to a mailing list, I do not sell or share the details, and I do not pass them to anyone else unless you ask me to.

Analytics

The site can count visits using Google Analytics, but only if you agree when asked. If you decline, or ignore the question, no analytics script loads at all. If you agree, the data I see is aggregate: which pages get read, roughly where visitors come from, and which devices they use. IP addresses are anonymised. You can change your mind by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

What is stored in your browser

  • Your light or dark theme choice, so the site does not flip on you between visits.
  • Your answer to the analytics question, so you are only asked once.

Both are stored locally in your own browser. Neither is sent to me or to anyone else.

Fonts and hosting

Typefaces are served by Google Fonts and the site is hosted on Vercel. Both providers receive standard request information such as your IP address and browser type as a technical necessity of serving the page. Their own privacy policies govern that.

How long I keep things

Enquiry emails stay in my inbox as business correspondence. If you would like your message and details deleted, email me and I will remove them and confirm once it is done.

Reaching me

Email davidakin6@gmail.com with any question about data on this site, including requests to see, correct or delete anything I hold about you.
